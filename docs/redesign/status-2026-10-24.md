## What changed today: the course-platform redesign

The user rejected the Part E look as childish and asked for a professional course platform: Coursera/Forage structure, Apple glass and roundness, real desktop layouts, and the MRT lines as the thing that sets Finline apart. The canvas was iterated with the user, comment by comment, and then built into the app.

- **Design system** (`app/tokens.css`):
  - 錢途 paper and ink: #F2F3F4 and #14171A.
  - Taipei Metro red, blue, green and yellow, and the four-color stripe.
  - Glass on navigation and controls only.
  - Radii of 24 and 32.
  - Noto Sans TC bold as the voice font, replacing the rounded Huninn.
  - A dark departure-board style with amber text.
- **App shell:** a glass sidebar on desktop (brand, 今天, 狀態, 路線, 行動, 設定) and a floating glass tab bar on phone.
- **今天:**
  - a greeting with the streak;
  - a "continue" card for the next station;
  - a life-sim card (month, cash, emergency fund, savings, stress);
  - the route board: lines being ridden as rows with progress bars, one open line showing its stations, and the rest as badges in four zones (日常的錢, 保護自己, 讓錢長大, 人生大事).
- **Course page** (`/line/[slug]`):
  - a header with the line's station sign and a board (stations, minutes, difficulty);
  - the stations on one continuous line;
  - a glass progress panel with skills, the certificate, 學習紀錄用語, interview tips, and the pre/post tests.
  - **上車** opens MetroRide: drag the 學習卡 onto the reader (or press 直接感應), it beeps, and an MRT car crosses the blurred screen with the line name and 「出發囉！」. It has 跳過, a reduced-motion version, and keyboard access.
- **Lesson player:**
  - one glass bar with the named stops 想一想, 先懂, 任務, 對答案, 人生;
  - the hook is a centered card;
  - the brief is a task ticket;
  - on desktop, the document is a white sheet beside a glass question pane.
- **Life-sim turn:** the same glass bar; the ride to the next station is the MRT car.
- **Landing page** (`/`), as station stops FL01 to FL05:
  - a headline beside a departure board, whose lines, stations and minutes come from the content;
  - four line cards;
  - a station's five stops;
  - 這裡不是美國, with the figures 0.1%, 6%, 3 方 and 15%, the same ones the lessons teach;
  - an interactive fare gate with a flashing 「滑動刷卡 SWIPE TO BOARD」 hint. Swiping in leads to sign-up.
- **Site header and sign-up:**
  - the site header is a floating glass pill;
  - sign-up uses the 「拿一張學習卡」 layout;
  - 用代碼登入 goes to `/signup#login`.
- **Avatar out of the main flow:**
  - onboarding is now the goal step, then 今天;
  - the character is gone from the hook, the reveal and the turn;
  - the 修改角色 links are removed.
  - `/creator` and the avatar data still exist, which leaves room for a later optional profile setup.
