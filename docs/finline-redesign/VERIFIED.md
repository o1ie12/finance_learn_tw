## 1. Deposit insurance: VERIFIED

- **Cap:** 「存款保險最高保額自民國100年1月1日起提高為新臺幣300萬元。」 (CDIC FAQ Q7)
- **Who it covers and in which currencies:** 「最高保額是指每一存款人，在國內同一家要保機構新臺幣及外幣存款之本金及利息受到存款保險保障的最高額度。」 (CDIC FAQ Q7)
- **Principal and interest share the cap:** 存款保險條例 §13: 「…係指每一存款人，在同一要保機構存款本金及迄最後營業日之利息受到存款保險保障之最高金額，存保公司履行保險責任時並以新臺幣為支付幣別。」
- **The amount itself is not in the statute.** §13 leaves it to the regulators: 「由主管機關會同財政部、中央銀行定之」.
- **What counts:** only deposits inside Taiwan (§12, 「中華民國境內之存款」). OBU deposits are excluded.
- **Not covered** (CDIC FAQ Q20): structured products, insurance policies, funds, stocks, ETFs, bonds, gold passbooks and virtual assets, 「無論該等商品是否標榜為保本」. Also excluded: negotiable CDs, and government, central-bank and interbank deposits.
- **Sources:**
  - https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0380039&flno=13 (存款保險條例 §13; also flno=12)
  - https://www.cdic.gov.tw/main_deposit/faq.aspx?uid=59&pid=59 (存款保險常見問答, page updated 2026-10-21)
  - https://www.cdic.gov.tw/main_estimate/faq.aspx (保額試算專區 問與答)
- **May say:** each depositor is covered up to NT$3,000,000 per insured bank, interest included, NT$ and foreign-currency deposits combined. Money at a second insured bank is covered separately; the per-institution wording 「同一要保機構」 implies this, though no page states it outright. Funds, stocks, insurance and crypto are not deposits and are not covered, even when sold at a bank.
- **Must not say:** 「法律規定 300 萬」, or that foreign-currency deposits get an extra cap of their own.

## 2. Free credit report: VERIFIED

- **Free copies:** one free copy a year online and one on paper, counted separately: 「每年度(1月1日至12月31日止)首次免費次數係分別計算」.
- **Prices after that:**
  - Online: 「同年度第2次以後每次查閱酌收新台幣80元」.
  - Paper: 「第2次(含加查)以後開始將酌收部分成本費用，中文版每次一份新台幣100元」.
- **Online access needs age 18:** 「申請人需年滿18歲且持有自然人憑證IC卡或軟體金融憑證，始能使用本系統線上查閱信用報告。」
- **Paper requests:** through the post office, in person, or by mail.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.jcic.org.tw/main_ch/report_2_1.aspx?uid=1659&pid=47 (如何取得信用報告)
  - https://www.jcic.org.tw/main_ch/docDetail.aspx?uid=451&pid=168&docid=474 (個人申請費用說明, 2025-03-27)
  - https://www.jcic.org.tw/main_ch/docDetail.aspx?uid=170&pid=168&docid=412 (個人線上查閱信用報告, 2025-12-23)
- **May say:** one free report a year online and one on paper. After that, NT$80 online or NT$100 on paper (Chinese). Online is for age 18 and up, so most high-school students will use it later.
- **Must not say:** "one free report a year in total", or that online needs a 自然人憑證 only.

## 3. The 22x unsecured-debt rule (DBR22): VERIFIED, and it is not a statute

- **Who set it:** 金管會, on 94/12/19, on the bankers' association's suggestion: 「金融機構對於債務人於全體金融機構之無擔保債務歸戶後之總餘額除以平均月收入，不宜超過22倍」 (Banking Bureau press release, 2024-08-08).
- **What it covers:** total unsecured debt across all institutions 「（包括信用卡、現金卡及信用貸款）」 (FSC letter 金管銀（四）字第09600523370號). Listed exclusions apply.
- **Not in any statute.** 信用卡業務機構管理辦法 §22 only says 「應依主管機關規定辦理」.
- **Still current:** since 2024, banks may apply to trial a financial-assessment model in place of the monthly-income test. No change to DBR22 itself was found.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.banking.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=540&parentpath=0&mcustomize=news_view.jsp&dataserno=202408080002&dtable=News
  - https://law.fsc.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FE052046
  - https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=G0380055
- **May say:** 「金管會規範（DBR22倍）：你在所有金融機構的無擔保債務（信用卡、現金卡、信貸）加總，原則上不宜超過平均月收入的22倍。」 Secured loans (mortgages) are outside it.
- **Must not say:** 「法律規定」 or 「銀行法規定」, or that it is only a bankers' association rule. Do not present it as an absolute ban: the wording is 「不宜」, and exceptions are approved.

## 4. Joint vs general guarantor: VERIFIED

- **General guarantor (先訴抗辯權):** 民法 §745: 「保證人於債權人未就主債務人之財產強制執行而無效果前，對於債權人得拒絕清償。」
- **When the right is lost** (民法 §746): the guarantor waives it, the main debtor is declared bankrupt, or the main debtor's property cannot cover the debt.
- **Joint guarantor:** 「債權人得無須先對主債務人訴追，即可直接向連帶保證人請求履行全部債務，因此，連帶保證人沒有先訴抗辯權。」 (FSC 金融小百科, citing 民法 §272, §273)
- **Bank rule:** 銀行法 §12-1: 「銀行辦理自用住宅放款及消費性放款，不得要求借款人提供連帶保證人。」 With adequate collateral, no guarantor may be required at all.
- **Sources:**
  - https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0000001&flno=745 (also 746, 272, 273)
  - https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0380001&flno=12-1
  - https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=940&parentpath=0%2C5&mcustomize=cyclopedia_view.jsp&dataserno=541
  - https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=940&parentpath=0%2C5&mcustomize=cyclopedia_view.jsp&dataserno=542
- **May say:** a general guarantor can make the lender go after the borrower first. A joint guarantor cannot: the lender can ask them for the whole debt straight away. Banks may not require a joint guarantor for consumer loans or home loans for your own use.
- **Must not say:** that banks can never ask for any guarantor on a consumer loan (a general guarantor is allowed without adequate collateral), or that a guarantor pays only after the borrower goes bankrupt.

## 5. Debt negotiation (前置協商): VERIFIED; the "only above 22x income" condition is REFUTED

- **Legal basis:** 消債條例 §151(1): 「債務人對於金融機構負債務者，在聲請更生或清算前，應向最大債權金融機構請求協商債務清償方案…」.
- **Who qualifies:** a 消費者 under §2 who owes financial institutions and has trouble repaying. The bankers' association's 作業準則 sets **no debt threshold** and no income multiple. The NT$12 million ceiling in §42 applies to court 更生, not to negotiation.
- **How:** get the creditor list from 聯徵中心 and apply to the largest creditor bank.
- **Result:** only unsecured bank debts are covered. 「期數 = 無擔保總債務金額 / 每月最低可還款金額」. Plans over 180 periods split into stages, the first at 「第一階段利率 0％」. Otherwise 「清償方案之利率以不高於原債權平均利率為原則」. There is no fixed rate cap.
- **Credit record** (作業準則 ch. 11; JCIC): a note while repaying; 「履約完成日或提前清償日加1年」 after paying off; 「自毀諾日起加3年」 if the plan is broken. The association's FAQ: 「進入前置協商、更生、清算程序後會有不良信用紀錄」.
- **Sources:**
  - https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=B0010042&flno=151 (also 2, 42)
  - https://www.ba.org.tw/memberStandard/details?id=90 (作業準則, 115.01.29 version)
  - https://www.twidrp.org.tw/p01.jsp (association FAQ)
  - https://www.jcic.org.tw/main_ch/List.aspx?uid=1617&pid=1577
- **May say:** if you owe banks and cannot keep up, contact the lender early. You can apply once, to your largest creditor bank, for a repayment plan. A plan can stretch up to about 15 years, at no more than your old average rate. It leaves a note on your credit record while you repay and for a year after you finish; breaking the plan leaves a note for 3 years.
- **Must not say:** that you qualify only above 22x income; a fixed 5% rate; that it erases debt; that it covers loans from family or friends; that it leaves no mark.

## 7. Insurance roles: VERIFIED

- 保險法 §3: 「本法所稱要保人，指對保險標的具有保險利益，向保險人申請訂立保險契約，並負有交付保險費義務之人。」
- 保險法 §4: 「本法所稱被保險人，指於保險事故發生時，遭受損害，享有賠償請求權之人；要保人亦得為被保險人。」
- 保險法 §5: 「本法所稱受益人，指被保險人或要保人約定享有賠償請求權之人，要保人或被保險人均得為受益人。」
- **Plain wording** (FSC 金融智慧網): 要保人 is 「向保險人投保之人，也是繳交保險費者。」
- **Sources:**
  - https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=G0390002&flno=3 (also 4, 5)
  - https://moneywise.fsc.gov.tw/tabf/FW/FW11_page_G.html
  - https://rm.ib.gov.tw/pages/Insu.aspx?Dir=001000&PKID=3be82cc9-6a7e-4aae-a2fc-164d4d1a25df
- **May say:** the 要保人 signs and pays the premiums; the 被保險人 is the one covered, who suffers the loss; the 受益人 is whoever is named to receive the payout. One person can hold two or all three roles.
- **Must not say:** that the three must be different people, or that the 受益人 is always the one who pays.

## 6. Scooter insurance layers: VERIFIED (mandatory cover and liability); top-up names PARTLY VERIFIED

- **Mandatory (強制汽車責任保險).** It pays injury and death of passengers and third parties, whoever was at fault:
  - 「使用或管理汽車致乘客或車外第三人傷害或死亡之事故」 (法 §13)
  - 「不論加害人有無過失」 (§7)
  - It covers scooters (§5). Owners must buy it (§6). An uninsured scooter is fined 「新臺幣一千五百元以上三千元以下罰鍰」 (§49).
- **Amounts since 115/7/1** (給付標準, amended 115/5/29):
  - Medical: 「每一受害人每一事故之傷害醫療費用給付總額，以新臺幣二十萬元為限」.
  - Death: 「死亡給付為每一人新臺幣三百萬元」.
  - Disability: 3,000,000 at grade 1 down to 80,000 at grade 15.
  - Per victim per accident, the total cap went from 2,200,000 to 3,200,000 (FSC 2026-05-28). **Do not use the old NT$2,000,000 figure.**
- **No property damage:** the mandatory cover 「不包含財物損失」 (FSC).
- **Your own injury in a single-vehicle crash:** the rider 「不屬於本法所稱的受害人，因此不能依本法申請理賠」 (FSC 2012-03-06). The FSC suggests 任意傷害保險 instead.
- **Third-party liability (汽車第三人責任保險):** covers third parties' 「體傷、死亡或財物損害」 (保險局 2025-10-30). The FSC model contract applies to scooters.
- **Top-ups:**
  - Own-vehicle damage: 車體損失保險.
  - Theft: 「汽車竊盜損失保險條款」, which also applies to scooters.
  - Driver injury: named only generically as 任意傷害保險. The exact scooter product names were not confirmed on an official page.
- **Sources:**
  - https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=G0390060
  - https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=G0390067
  - https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0%2C2&mcustomize=news_view.jsp&dataserno=202605280001&dtable=News
  - https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0,2&mcustomize=news_view.jsp&dataserno=201203060004&dtable=News
  - https://law.fsc.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL047990
  - https://www.ib.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=239&parentpath=0%2C2%2C238&mcustomize=news_view.jsp&dataserno=202510300001&dtable=News
- **May say:**
  - The law requires every scooter owner to buy 強制險. It pays only for people's injuries and deaths, not property: up to NT$200,000 medical and up to NT$3,000,000 for death per person.
  - It never pays to fix any vehicle, and in a crash with no other vehicle the rider's own injuries are not covered.
  - 第三人責任險, 車體險, 竊盜險 and driver-injury cover are optional.
- **Must not say:** that 強制險 covers bike or property damage; that it always covers the rider; the old NT$2,000,000 figure.

## 8. TISA (臺灣個人投資儲蓄帳戶): PARTLY VERIFIED

- **Status (FSC 2026-10-22):**
  - The first phase started in July 2025: 「採基金先行策略，由投信業者讓利發行TISA級別基金」.
  - There are more than 270,000 accounts.
  - The tax break is only 「初步規劃」 and still needs 行政院 and 立法院 steps.
  - As of May 2026: 「由25家投信提供45檔基金」.
- **Rules, from the 臺灣集中保管結算所 FAQ** (the operator the FSC directs, not an FSC page):
  - Minimum: 「僅能透過TISA帳戶進行定期定額申購，最低申購金額新台幣1,000元」.
  - Fees: 「免收申購手續費」, 「經理費低於1%」; banks may add their own trust fees.
  - 24-month rule: 「須連續申購24個月，若未滿…自終止、贖回或扣款失敗之日起6個月內…不得『新增』定期定額申購契約」.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0,2&mcustomize=news_view.jsp&dataserno=202610220004&dtable=News
  - https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0%2C2&mcustomize=news_view.jsp&dataserno=202606160001&dtable=News
  - https://m.tdcc.com.tw/TDCCWEB/upload/4028c0b396fc8f590197865d135b006b.pdf
- **May say:** TISA started in mid-2025 with selected low-fee funds bought by regular debits from NT$1,000 a purchase, no subscription fee. Stopping before 24 straight months blocks restarting that fund for 6 months. There is no tax break yet.
- **Must not say:** that a tax deduction exists now; that it is risk-free; a fixed list of banks; that the fee "is 1%".
- **Still needed:** the minimum, fee and 24-month figures confirmed on an FSC or SFB page. Until then the TISA deep station stays out (06 section 2).

## 9. Online certificate as a 學習歷程檔案 多元表現 item (06 F3.5, D3): limits VERIFIED; online certificates UNVERIFIED

- **Application limits** (招聯會, 115學年度 申請入學):
  - 課程學習成果: 「提供至多3件」.
  - 多元表現: 「至多10件，並須另行上傳多元表現綜整心得(字數合計至多800字…)」.
  - Categories include 「F.高中自主學習計畫與成果」 and 「K.非修課紀錄之成果作品」.
- **Uploading** (教育部): 多元表現 「無需教師認證，可以外部連結方式上傳」. Students tick at most 10 a year. Only items earned while in high school count.
- **Online self-study certificates: UNVERIFIED.** No 教育部, 國教署 or 招聯會 page names them. Whether one helps is up to each 校系.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.cac.edu.tw/apply115/system/ColQry_115xappLyfOrStu_Azd5gP29/html/Apply_ReviewData.pdf
  - https://www.edu.tw/News_Content.aspx?n=9E7AC85F1954DDA8&s=BE0D4D71E50F6AC8
  - https://ws.moe.edu.tw/001/Upload/23/relfile/8006/55624/274d7544-905b-4b43-af8a-9d7c0428a14c.pdf
- **May say (only after D3):** a student can upload a completion certificate as a 多元表現 item within their school's window.
- **Must not say:** that it is officially recognised, that it improves admission chances, or that it counts as 課程學習成果. The F3.5 snippet stays a draft labelled 「線上自主學習，非實習或工作經驗」.
